🏢 About Us

Introduction &
Company Overview

LQUOM is a quantum communication company developing quantum repeater systems to address the fundamental distance limitations and scalability of quantum networks. Rather than treating these challenges as purely theoretical, the company focuses on building physically realizable systems that combine entangled photon sources, quantum memories, and precise synchronization technologies.

At the core of LQUOM's approach is the integration of cavity-enhanced entangled photon sources, rare-earth-ion-based quantum memories, and optical frequency and phase control. The difficulty is not in any single component, but in maintaining coherence and timing across the entire system.

Founded by researchers with backgrounds in quantum optics and photonic systems, LQUOM brings together expertise in experimental physics, laser engineering, and system integration.

💡 Motivation

Why This Problem
Matters

In fiber-based quantum communication, photon loss increases exponentially with distance, making it fundamentally difficult to transmit quantum states or entanglement over long distances. Unlike classical communication, quantum signals cannot be amplified along the way.

Quantum repeaters are a proposed solution to overcome this limitation. However, a quantum repeater is not a single device. It is a systems-level challenge that requires the integration of:
• Entangled photon sources
• Quantum memories
• Frequency and phase synchronization
• Interference and measurement systems

Key Insight: In this problem, you are not asked whether something is theoretically possible. You are asked whether it can be implemented as a working system.

🎯 Expected Learning Outcomes
  • What the true bottlenecks of photon transmission are
  • Why systems integration is more difficult than individual components
  • Which architectures are physically realistic
  • How quantum repeaters impact quantum communication

🔑 Key Challenges
  • Bell State Measurement Efficiency
    How can the efficiency of Bell State measurement be improved by more than 50%?
  • Entanglement Purification
    What is required in entanglement purification?
  • Quantum Memory Improvement
    How can this type of quantum memory be improved to be more efficient and have a greater number of modes? (from the aspect of control laser and the physical properties of rare-earth ions)
  • Entanglement-Based QKD vs BB84
    How can entanglement-based QKD outperform BB84 in realistic scenarios? (e.g., 100 km, 500 km, 1000 km)

🛠️ Design Challenges

Engineering
Design Tasks

  • Magnetic System Design
    Design a magnetic system in our quantum memory system inside a vacuum chamber.
  • Compact Entangled Photon Source
    Design a compact optical system of a narrow linewidth (linewidth < 5 MHz) entangled photon source. "Compact" corresponds to a 400 mm × 300 mm × 110 mm area.
  • Compact Quantum Memory System
    Design a compact optical system of quantum memory. "Compact" corresponds to a 400 mm × 300 mm × 110 mm area.
