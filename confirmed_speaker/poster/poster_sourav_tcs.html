Speaker Bio
Dr. Sourav Chatterjee is a Research Engineer at TCS Innovation Labs, Pune, specializing in quantum computing, quantum sensing, and quantum communication. He is a doctoral candidate in Physics with expertise in photonic Ising machines, secure quantum communication, and quantum metrology. His work at TCS explores quantum sensing for industrial applications in healthcare, electronics, and magnetic material analysis. Sourav is driven by curiosity and enjoys finding practical industrial use cases for quantum technologies that can create real-world impact.
