## 📝 Problem Statement — Action Required

As part of the event's theme — *"What does your company/organisation want from the quantum workforce and research community?"* — we request each speaker to share a **brief problem statement or key question** that your organisation would like academia to address.

This could be:
- A specific **technical challenge** your team is working on.
- A **skill gap** you see in fresh graduates or researchers.
- An **open research question** where academic collaboration would be valuable.
- A **product/market need** that quantum research could address.

If you have already shared this with us, thank you! If not, please feel free to **share a v1 draft now** — it doesn't need to be final. You can always modify or refine it later. We will feature these problem statements on our event page and promotional materials to maximise engagement.

**Please send your problem statement to:** [shashankg@iiti.ac.in](mailto:shashankg@iiti.ac.in)
