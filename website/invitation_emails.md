## Email 1 — Dr. Randy Kuang (Quantropi)

**To:** invest@quantropi.com  
**Subject:** Invitation to Speak — "What Quantum Industry Wants?" | IIT Indore × National Quantum Mission

Dear Randy,

It's been a while since we last connected, I hope you are doing well.

I am writing to invite you as a speaker to our upcoming event, **"What Quantum Industry Wants?"**, to be held on **14 April 2026 (World Quantum Day)** via Google Meet, under the aegis of the **IIT Indore × National Quantum Mission, India**. The idea is to give an insight on the industrial requirements that students should nurture.

Your pioneering work in **quantum-safe cryptography** at Quantropi — with 30+ patents and deep expertise in post-quantum security — aligns perfectly with the objectives of this initiative. We believe your insights would be invaluable for our students and the broader academic-industry community.

**About the Event:**  
This event aims to bridge the gap between quantum industry and academia by making students industry-ready before placement/joining. We invite industry leaders to present the exciting product or solution they are developing and share a **problem statement** that interests engineering undergraduate students to work on over the summer. Students who successfully solve the problem statement may receive an **internship or PPO** from your organization. We expect around **1500 student participants** and **50 quantum industry partners**.

The problem statement (PS) will be shared on a dedicated Discord channel where students can collaborate, seek mentorship, and deliver solutions over the summer. The PS will be categorised under quantum computing, communication, sensing, metrology and materials.

**What We Need from You:**
1. A 20–30 minute presentation on what Quantropi is doing? and challenge or opportunities going forward.
2. A completed problem statement using the attached template (see attachment: *Problem Statement Template*).

I have attached the event poster, and the problem statement template for your reference.

We would be honored to have you as our event speaker. Please let me know your availability, and I will be happy to coordinate the logistics. In case you are not available, a pre recorded video will also serve the purpose.

Warm regards,  
**Dr. Shashank Gupta**  
Assistant Professor  
Department of Physics & ICQC  
IIT Indore  
Email: shashankg@iiti.ac.in
