\section*{1.\quad Executive Summary}
\vspace{-4pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

This proposal seeks \textbf{internal funding of \textrm{₹}1,80,000} from IIT Indore to organise the \textbf{``What Quantum Industry Wants?''} --- a one-day virtual conclave on \textbf{14 April 2026 (World Quantum Day)}, bringing together 11 confirmed speakers from the quantum industry to interact with IIT Indore's students and faculty.

The event will position IIT Indore as a hub for quantum technology discourse, strengthen the institute's industry linkages, and enhance placement and internship opportunities in the emerging quantum sector.

% ===============================================
\section*{2.\quad Rationale}
\vspace{-4pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

\subsection*{2.1\quad Why This Event?}

IIT Indore is actively building a quantum computing ecosystem through its \textbf{Inter-disciplinary Centre for Quantum Computing (ICQC)} and NQM-funded research programmes. However, students and researchers often lack direct exposure to:

\begin{itemize}
  \item What quantum companies are \textbf{actually building} and what skills they value.
  \item The \textbf{career landscape} in quantum computing, cryptography, and sensing.
  \item How academic research \textbf{translates to industry products} and services.
\end{itemize}

This event addresses these gaps by bringing 11 industry leaders --- from global giants (D-Wave, IBM, Deloitte) to emerging Indian startups (Quanfluence, Taqbit, GDQLabs, QNu Labs) --- directly to our students.

\subsection*{2.2\quad Why World Quantum Day?}

14 April is internationally recognised as \textbf{World Quantum Day}, celebrated globally to promote quantum science awareness. Hosting this event on World Quantum Day will:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Amplify IIT Indore's visibility in the quantum technology community.
  \item Align with global celebrations, attracting wider participation and media coverage.
  \item Demonstrate the institute's commitment to cutting-edge technology engagement.
\end{itemize}
