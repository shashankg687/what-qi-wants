\section*{1.\quad Executive Summary}
\vspace{-4pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

We propose a one-day virtual industry--academia conclave titled \textbf{``What Quantum Industry Wants?''} to be held on \textbf{14 April 2026} --- \emph{World Quantum Day}. The event aims to bridge the gap between India's quantum research ecosystem and the rapidly evolving quantum industry by bringing together 11 confirmed speakers from leading quantum companies and government bodies.

This proposal seeks financial support of \textbf{\textrm{₹}2,70,000} from the \textbf{Quantum Mission Division (QMD), National Quantum Mission (NQM)}, to cover speaker honoraria, platform costs, publicity, and event management.

% ===============================================
\section*{2.\quad Background \& Motivation}
\vspace{-4pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

India's National Quantum Mission (NQM) --- a \textrm{₹}6,003 crore initiative --- is driving the nation toward global quantum leadership across computing, communication, sensing, and materials. While significant progress has been made in academic research and mission-mode projects, there remains a critical need to:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Understand what the \textbf{quantum industry expects} from the academic talent pipeline.
  \item Identify \textbf{skill gaps} and emerging job roles in quantum technologies.
  \item Foster \textbf{direct dialogue} between quantum startups, MNCs, and university researchers.
  \item Showcase \textbf{NQM's impact} in catalysing India's quantum ecosystem.
\end{itemize}

The event is strategically timed with World Quantum Day to maximise visibility and align with global quantum awareness campaigns.

% ===============================================
\section*{3.\quad Objectives}
\vspace{-4pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Provide a platform for quantum industry leaders to articulate their \textbf{technology roadmaps, hiring needs, and collaboration priorities}.
  \item Enable students and early-career researchers to understand \textbf{industry expectations and career pathways}.
  \item Highlight the role of \textbf{NQM} in enabling quantum technology development in India.
  \item Create a \textbf{recorded knowledge repository} of industry perspectives for future reference.
  \item Strengthen the \textbf{IIT Indore--NQM partnership} in quantum workforce development.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{7.\quad Expected Outcomes}
\vspace{-4pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{500+ participants} including students, researchers, and industry professionals from across India.
  \item \textbf{Recorded sessions} to serve as a lasting resource for quantum workforce development.
  \item \textbf{Industry--academia networking} leading to potential internships, collaborations, and sponsored projects.
